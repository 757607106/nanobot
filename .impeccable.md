## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience:** Ordinary users using the application as an everyday companion assistant during their daily work.
- **Context:** Strictly desktop browser environment (mobile compatibility is discontinued and to be removed).
- **Job to be Done:** Efficiently interact with nanobot for assistance, scheduling, coding, and information retrieval.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone:** Refined, Smart, Luxurious.
- **References:** Apple (meticulous detailing, fluid motion), Google (clarity, material-inspired hierarchy), OpenAI GPT (clean typography, focus on content).
- **Emotional Goals:** Evoke a sense of "Tech Luxury" (科技奢华感) - feeling premium, effortless, and highly capable.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Primary Theme:** Light mode by default, gracefully adapting to Dark mode for night usage.
- **Visual Tone:** Elegant, highly polished, with excellent typographic hierarchy and spacing.
- **Anti-references:** Cluttered layouts, noisy borders, generic "AI slop" widgets, mobile-first squished elements.

### Design Principles
1. **Focus on Desktop-Class Layouts:** Exploit wide screens with generous margins, complex grid structures, and asynchronous layouts. Strip out all legacy mobile-responsive compromises.
2. **Tech Luxury through Typography & Space:** Rely on premium font scaling, meticulous line-heights, and breathable whitespace rather than borders and cards to define structure. 
3. **Intentional Nuance:** Use subtle color tints (oklch) instead of pure white/black, and rely on smooth, exponential easing for interactions to mirror Apple/OpenAI's high-end feel.
4. **Fluid Context Switching:** Soft, seamless transitions between Light and Dark mode, ensuring neither theme feels like an afterthought.
